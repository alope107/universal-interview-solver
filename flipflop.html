A flip-flop in CSS. No script, no hover, no checkbox. The bit lives in the browser's last remembered size; an animation is the clock; container queries are the wiring.

Expected: the grey box is A wide by B tall and walks the corners every second: 60×60, 20×60, 20×20, 60×20, repeat. Green lamp = A, blue lamp = B; their ring darkens on phase 1.

If the ring never changes, custom-property keyframes or style queries aren't supported here. If the ring ticks but the box is stuck, the loop isn't closing: a box frozen at 60×60 means A's remembered width isn't being used; a box that collapses to 20×20 or 0 means the remembered size isn't being recorded.
